An extensive research in Lidar properties for robotic applications

Project Description

This is an individual research project that studies the properties of a RP12 Lidar. The experiment involves collecting Lidar data in the form of point clouds using white and black boards. The data were analyzed and compared to assess the suitability of the Lidar for robotics applications.

From both the graphical and video analysis of the data, it was concluded that the lidar may have limitations in detecting small spacings during its operation. But In general, the lidar should be expected to perform well in environment mapping while struggle slightly with small or multi-angled objects.

Leorover mobile robot project

Workspace charter Prelminary design review Final design review

Project Description

The project is to design and develop a fully autonomous robot capable of navigating a rectangular search area with randomly placed obstacles to collect 3 coloured cylinders and return them to matching-coloured bins at the rover’s starting point within a 20-minute mission time.

Our mobile robot offers a compact design and is suitable for open space exploration and salvation tasks involving multiple small objects. It is especially useful in environment that could be hazardous to human workers. The robot uses economic materials and a modular design which allows for any consumers, researchers or hobbiests to work on. In summary, our robot is suitable for both industrial applications as well as laboratory researching.

My Role

I was responsible for the design and implementation of the robotic manipulation function of the rover to perform pick and place tasks of cylinder objects. I designed a custom trajectory planning algorithm that enables the manipulator to smoothly perform pick and place operations. I also wrotes the power system summary for the design review.

In term of team coordination I constantly helps other groups work if possible and offers constructive opinions. I always aims to complete my task to the best quality and aim to do more if possible.

Autonomous drone control design project

Project Description

This is a small group project. The objective of this project is to design and develop the control system of a fully autonomous drone robot capable of navigating its environment by flying to pre-planned target points. The drone uses a cascade PID controller with gain scheduling and disturbance rejection. It achieves robust and stable performance in both simulation and real drone experiments.

My Role

I was resposible for writing controller code, tuning parameters and testing the drone is simulation and in laboratory. The project requires frequent collaboration and code sharing for effective communication. I was also collecting data and taking expderiment videos for further data processing and analysis.
